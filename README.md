UltraRogue is a dungeon game in the line of Rogue, written by Herb
Chong. You choose one of ten character classes, from fighter to ninja,
and go down through a hundred levels of rooms, mazes and throne rooms,
past trading posts and monsters that cast spells and summon others, to
bring back the Amulet of Yendor and the other artifacts.

This is UltraRogue 1.05, dated December 2, 1993.

What is different from 1.04:

- Sixteen new monsters, among them the snake, the giant beetle, the
  killer frog, Yeenoghu and the titanothere, and the devil Asmodeus in
  place of the arch devil. Many others change letter or damage: bats are
  B, hobgoblins H, floating eyes E, wraiths W, vampires V.
- Weapons and armor go by Rogue's names and dice: the two handed sword,
  mace, spear and long sword, leather armor and studded leather armor.
- The Wand of Lucifer is the Wand of Yendor.
- A ring of teleportation moves its wearer twice as often, the trip to a
  throne room comes ten times more rarely, and minor artifacts do nothing
  less often.
- The short "terse" messages are gone and several messages are reworded.
- The source is ANSI C.
